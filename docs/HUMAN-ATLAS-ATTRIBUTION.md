# Human Atlas attribution

Human Atlas adapts the MIT-licensed Human Atlas application by ashemag: https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas . Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag. The MIT license notice is preserved in the included viewer assets.

Anatomy data: BodyParts3D 4.0, © Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0. Source: https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/ and https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html . The browser data is an adapted, performance-optimized adult male reference anatomy; it does not represent all bodies, ages, sexes, structures, or variation.

Khizooology hosts the static viewer locally, adds Infooo discovery, surrounding educational context, and the public attribution surface. It is for education and exploration, not diagnosis or treatment.
