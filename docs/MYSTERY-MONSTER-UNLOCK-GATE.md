# Mystery Monster Unlock Gate

**Purpose first → monster second.** A top-level monster exists only when a new kind of visitor value keeps appearing and no active monster can own it well. Empty sections, mascot art, naming patterns, traffic potential, and an unused mystery slot are never enough.

## Internal lifecycle

`MYSTERY → CANDIDATE → PROTOTYPE → READY TO UNLOCK → ACTIVE`

These are governance stages, not additions to the public monster-status model.

- **Mystery:** no identity, purpose, promise, date, feature list, or public route beyond the shared noindex mystery surface.
- **Candidate:** internal problem, visitor benefit, territory test, and possible flagship only.
- **Prototype:** private/local validation of usefulness, distinctness, depth, and interaction or content quality.
- **Ready to Unlock:** every gate below has passed and a release can be prepared.
- **Active:** an approved public launch with identity, route, flagship, navigation, voice, truthful SEO, accessibility, mobile, privacy, and performance readiness.

A candidate may be **held** or **dropped** at any stage. This is a successful outcome when an existing monster can own the work, the value is thin, the territory is not repeatable, or the flagship is not strong enough. Stopping investment never automatically deletes or unpublishes an active monster.

## Unlock gate

Before Khizar considers an unlock, answer all of these clearly:

1. **Purpose:** What unique job does it perform for a visitor in one sentence?
2. **Distinct territory:** Why can Artooo, Toolooo, Infooo, or Notooo not own that job?
3. **Repeatability:** Can it produce more coherent, valuable work without filler?
4. **Flagship:** What real, finished experience proves the territory now?
5. **Value Laws:** Does it pass Utility, Identity, and Truth hard gates, with a Strong-or-better result preferred?
6. **Evidence:** What repeated creator problem, user/search signal, related creation, Compound Value opportunity, or prototype result supports it? Low data stays uncertain.
7. **Identity:** Can role, purpose, personality, and visitor benefit be named without relying on color, mascot, or an `ooo` suffix?
8. **Relationship:** What leads into it, what does it connect back to, and how does it strengthen the existing universe?
9. **Publishing quality:** Is the complete launch ready today under the [Publishing Quality Checklist](PUBLISHING-QUALITY-CHECKLIST.md)?
10. **Reveal test:** Would keeping it mysterious truly be worse than revealing it?

If any answer is vague, **keep it mysterious**. No numeric winner score or automatic threshold replaces this review.

## Decision record

Keep this internal until an approved launch:

```text
CANDIDATE: internal only
PROBLEM: recurring need
VISITOR VALUE: clear benefit
WHY EXISTING MONSTERS CANNOT OWN IT: territory test
FLAGSHIP: finished proof
REPEATABILITY: credible future work
EVIDENCE: creator, prototype, or aggregate signals
VALUE LAW RESULT: reviewed result and hard gates
PUBLISHING GATE: ready / not ready
DECISION: hold / prototype / ready to unlock / drop
```

The [Build Smarter With Data](BUILD-SMARTER-WITH-DATA.md) workflow can inform evidence and effort, while [Compound Value](COMPOUND-VALUE-WORKFLOW.md) can show genuine connections. Neither can manufacture a purpose or trigger an unlock. Khizar’s explicit approval is required for every release.

## Mystery protection

Locked monsters use the canonical shared mystery voice only. Their public output stays anonymous: `???ooo`, `Unknown`, the shared future-monsters route, generic locked-creature alt text, and noindex metadata. Do not reveal historical names, candidate names, internal codes, roles, feature concepts, routes, launch dates, or individual personalities through HTML, metadata, structured data, image filenames, alt text, ARIA labels, manifests, social drafts, or links.

The mystery page may say that Khizooology is unfinished and that a real flagship must earn a name. It must not become a roadmap, countdown, feature teaser, or promise.

## Release boundary

An approved monster unlock is a complete release, not a name announcement. Apply the normal content-growth foundation: canonical identity and route, accurate metadata and schema, internal discovery, sitemap/indexability approval, real flagship, voice, relevant artwork, accessibility, mobile behavior, privacy, performance, and full validation. Do not add a new public monster, page, or sitemap entry before Khizar approves the release.
