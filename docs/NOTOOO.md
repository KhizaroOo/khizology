# Notooo — Book in One Page

**Role:** REMEMBER  
**Tagline:** One Book. One Page.  
**Supporting line:** Big knowledge. Small space. Simple words.

Notooo is Khizooology’s knowledge and notes monster. It researches, understands, filters, compresses, and visualizes the useful ideas from one important book as one simple knowledge page.

Its job is to preserve compressed knowledge worth returning to. Notooo is not a blog, a book review, a chapter-by-chapter summary, a quote collection, a generic article, or a way to create pages for SEO.

## Place in Khizooology

- **Artooo** helps people **FEEL**.
- **Toolooo** helps people **DO**.
- **Infooo** helps people **UNDERSTAND**.
- **Notooo** helps people **REMEMBER**.

Notooo owns ideas from books that are worth keeping. It must not blur into a Toolooo workflow or an Infooo interactive world.

## V1 format and categories

V1 has one format only: **Book in One Page**. Each entry turns one book into an A4 portrait visual knowledge page. The implementation may use HTML, CSS, and SVG; a real khizooo-made physical sheet may be added later. Neither approach may pretend to be a handwritten scan or claim that khizooo physically wrote it when that is not true.

The only primary categories are **Mind**, **Money**, **Nature**, **Life**, **People**, and **Society**. Use tags for deeper classification. Do not add another primary category without an intentional change to this document and the Notooo registry.

## Content contract

An entry usually includes a book and author, a simple big idea, four to seven key ideas, visual thinking where it clarifies relationships, a practical example when it earns its space, a clearly labelled **KHIZOOO TAKE**, and a final **REMEMBER →** idea.

The layout adapts to the book. Readability wins over decoration. Remove any idea that does not improve understanding or memory.

The central experience is **Discover → Understand → Download → Print → Keep**. Production pages should provide focused/fullscreen reading, a browser-generated high-resolution PNG download, and print support. Keep this browser-first, with no signup.

## Truth, research, and copyright

Use this visible, subtle disclosure on every production entry:

> A khizooo-curated one-page synthesis created with AI-assisted research. It captures useful ideas, not the complete book.

Also state that Notooo is not a replacement for reading the original book. Never claim khizooo read a complete book unless that is true.

Notooo is transformative interpretation: use original paraphrasing, visual synthesis, plain-language explanation, and original examples where useful. Do not copy chapters, long quotations, official cheat sheets, third-party summaries, or a book’s visual layout. Established framework names may be used when they are needed for accuracy.

## Production workflow

1. **Select** — khizooo chooses or approves a book.
2. **Research** — ChatGPT uses reliable sources, preferring the author, publisher, and other primary material.
3. **Understand** — identify the thesis, major ideas, useful frameworks, examples, and relevant limits.
4. **Compress** — remove repetition, filler, and secondary detail.
5. **Visualize** — create a page hierarchy that helps a reader remember.
6. **Curate** — khizooo reviews usefulness, simplicity, taste, and final direction.
7. **Implement** — Codex integrates the page, route, accessibility, print/download behavior, SEO, analytics, and performance.
8. **Verify** — check factual accuracy, clarity, mobile and keyboard UX, print/download, SEO, performance, and copyright discipline.

ChatGPT owns research, understanding, content, and compression. Codex owns implementation, integration, and verification. khizooo owns curation, taste, and final approval.

## Publishing gate

Ask: **If someone gives us only five minutes, is this page worth those five minutes?**

Do not publish material that is shallow, decorative-only, repetitive, misleading, padded for SEO, materially inaccurate, or primarily AI-generated noise. Optimize for **Clarity → Compression → Understanding → Memory**.

Add a public Notooo entry only after khizooo approves release. Reuse `src/data/notooo.ts` and the shared Notooo components; do not create page-specific parallel systems. Public entries need the complete Khizooology growth foundation: accurate metadata, canonical URL, social metadata, appropriate structured data, internal links, sitemap coverage, accessible assets, and the existing release checks.

The goal is a small collection of useful ideas worth keeping, one page at a time — not thousands of summaries.
